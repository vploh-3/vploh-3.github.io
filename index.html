Short Bio

Dr. Xiao Sui is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Advanced Study, Tsinghua University. She received her Ph.D. in Cyberspace Security from Shandong University in 2024 and her B.A. in Mathematics from Shandong University in 2019.

Research Interests

  • Byzantine fault tolerance and distributed consensus
  • Blockchain systems and secure distributed computation
  • Applied cryptography and multiparty computation
  • Composable and provably secure distributed protocols
